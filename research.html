STRATEGY RESEARCH / PAPER ONLY

Compare swing strategies and exits

Eight fixed comparisons: breakout or trend pullback, each with full, trailing, time or partial exits. First 70% is development; later 30% is a separately reported test. Warm-up uses earlier bars, never later prices. Results stay in research.

Need 120–5,000 completed, ordered bars. JSON format: [{"datetime":"2026-01-02 09:30:00","open":100,"high":101,"low":99,"close":100,"volume":1000}]. Timestamps are New York time. No extra data subscription or provider request. Repeated datasets return their original run; reused later periods are labeled. Cost estimates: 5 bps half-spread + 2 bps slippage, $1 fee and 35 bps FX allowance per side. These are assumptions, not broker charges.
