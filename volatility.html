The experiment

Use 20 completed daily returns to estimate annual volatility. Scale entry value and planned-loss limits by the smaller of 1 and 15% divided by that volatility. Freeze these caps at signal time; never increase quantity at the next open. Existing positions are not resized. Daily entries, SMA exits and protective stops retain their original timing.
