Trade costs versus funding costs

The primary funded model retains a conservative $1 fee reserve per order, with no per-trade FX charge. Conversion spreads apply once at funding and once at hypothetical withdrawal. The original per-trade allowance stays visible, and a separate stress case doubles execution reserves and conversion spreads.

The zero-brokerage sensitivity omits unverified pass-through fees and is a lower-cost bound, not an account-specific tariff. Funding uses a fixed illustrative reference of 0.655 USD per AUD. Results include no historical currency movements, taxes, distributions or interest. A quoted 0.005 spread is not a universal 0.5% fee.
