# What would make this a credible profitable operation?

The desk is a working paper operation. It is not a proven profitable system. Six daily development candidates failed the cost screen; the shared-account baseline lost $172.18 at normal estimated costs and $217.31 at doubled costs. That original assessment is historical; current ledger figures belong to the authenticated dashboard. A small gain or one closed trade cannot establish expectancy.

## First: resolve research inputs

The ETF Research page now explains input failures using the saved dataset. It groups issuer dividend events and adjustment-factor discrepancies by year, provides the first three discrepancy examples, and lists the evidence required next. It makes no provider calls, changes no stored experiment or gate, and never starts a performance calculation or promotes a strategy.

The saved IEF dataset contains 51 distribution discrepancies with little or no corresponding provider adjustment. This is consistent with missing adjustment coverage; it does not establish which source is correct. Verify raw price and event history independently before using it. IWM and EFA passed the existing checks. IAU needs complete distribution coverage plus independently recorded split verification. GSG needs distribution coverage and missing early price history. Missing records are never interpreted as zero events.

The issuer's [June 2010 IAU announcement](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1278680/000119312510137666/dex991.htm) specifies a ten-for-one split with split-adjusted trading beginning 24 June. That resolves the historical question, but it has not yet been incorporated into a complete verified action bundle. The original gate therefore stays blocked. GSG's [issuer annual filing](https://www.ishares.com/us/literature/annual-filings/gsg-1231.pdf) records its initial public trading in July 2006; a price series beginning in January 2008 cannot satisfy this experiment's declared coverage. Recent no-distribution statements do not certify the entire 2006–2015 period.

## Then: test the fixed diversified portfolio

Once every input passes, run the existing registered ETF baseline at normal and doubled costs. Retain failures. Do not replace failed symbols, shorten the period or tune parameters to obtain a pass. The portfolio shares the actual sizing, cash, exposure and circuit-breaker policy. Examine net expectancy in original R, drawdown, profit factor, trade count and dependence on the largest winners.

## Only then: independent validation and forward paper comparison

The existing promotion bar is 100 closed trades, at least +0.15R expectancy at doubled costs, profit factor at least 1.3, and acceptable profit concentration and drawdown. Passing development is not permission to trade: register validation first and distinguish previously viewed history from genuinely future observations. Compare a qualifying candidate with frozen v1 in paper mode; a failed validation stops that candidate. Do not increase risk to compensate for weak evidence.

## Separate operational gaps

Single-stock earnings exclusions still need a verified free, dated event source. USD funding/FX cashflows need account-specific evidence before replacing per-trade FX estimates. Actual AUD returns require dated conversions; no assumed after-tax profit is shown. These improvements cannot repair a signal that loses even before costs.
